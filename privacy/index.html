Sky Kitchen Privacy Policy

AUgames · Effective 29 September 2026

In short: Sky Kitchen does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics, and it does not need an internet connection to play.

Who we are

Sky Kitchen is a mobile game developed by AUgames ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and the game does not send any data to us.

Information stored on your device

To remember your game, Sky Kitchen saves a small amount of data only on your own device:

  • your progress through the levels,
  • in-game coins, lives and power-ups,
  • your settings, such as sound and music on or off.

This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your device's settings.

Third-party services

Unity. Sky Kitchen is made with the Unity game engine. The game uses none of Unity's online services — no Unity Analytics, Unity Ads or crash reporting — and sends no data to Unity Technologies.

Google Play. If you download Sky Kitchen from Google Play, or join a test of it, Google handles that download, your test membership and any feedback you send through Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy.

The game contains no advertising, analytics, social or payment services.

Children

Sky Kitchen is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Sky Kitchen adds a feature that handles personal data, such as advertising, analytics or in-app purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data can be sent to augames.info@gmail.com.
